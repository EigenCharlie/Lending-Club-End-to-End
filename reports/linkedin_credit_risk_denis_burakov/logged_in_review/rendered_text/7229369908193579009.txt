In a nutshell, gradient boosting is an ensemble technique where each new decision tree learns from the mistakes (pseudo-residuals) of the previous trees, iteratively improving the model’s predictions. Yet, despite this clear explanation, the process can still feel abstract 🌀 To bring this concept to life, I've created a visualization that demonstrates the dynamics of gradient boosting in action 🔎 ⤵ • Positive cases are marked with a ➕ symbol. • Negative cases are marked with a ➖ symbol. • The size of each symbol represents the residuals, i.e. how far off the model's prediction was from the true label. How it works 🔄 • Initially, the model may misclassify several examples, leading to large residuals (and thus larger symbols). • As the boosting process continues, the model adjusts predictions by adding new trees that correct these mistakes. • Over successive iterations, incorrectly classified examples with large residuals are gradually corrected, reducing their size and improving accuracy.
