In contrast, **logistic regression** is typically used in credit risk modeling to estimate the **probability of a single event**, such as default. These models treat risk as binary — default or no default — since, conceptually, there’s "no resurrection": a default is a terminal event. Even if further activity occurs post-default, it’s usually handled by separate models (e.g., LGD or EAD), not by repeating the original probability model.
