Recently I saw a paper claiming a 130% PR-AUC "breakthrough" in credit scoring from a foundation model, against an unnamed baseline. Meanwhile, more fundamental applied work stays under the radar in internal decks and unpublished notes. An Experian deck on "GINI Effect on Bad Rate/ Losses" is one I finally was able to work through cleanly. The idea is to model the score distribution as bi-normal: goods and bads each follow a normal distribution on the score scale, with unit variance and a separation σ between their means. Gini then maps to σ through the standard normal CDF: σ = √𝟤 × Φ⁻¹(𝖠𝖴𝖢), where 𝖠𝖴𝖢 = (𝖦𝗂𝗇𝗂 + 𝟣) / 𝟤 The conversion to AUC is needed because Φ⁻¹ expects a probability between 0 and 1. From Bayes, the acceptance rate of portfolio decomposes as: 𝖠𝖱 = 𝖯(𝖺𝖼𝖼𝖾𝗉𝗍 | 𝗀𝗈𝗈𝖽) × (𝟣 − 𝗉) + 𝖯(𝖺𝖼𝖼𝖾𝗉𝗍 | 𝖻𝖺𝖽) × 𝗉 where 𝗉 is the population bad rate. The bi-normal model gives you the two group-specific acceptance rates at any cutoff 𝖼: the fraction of each bell sitting to the right of 𝖼. That's all you need to reason about portfolio behavior. 𝗚𝗶𝗻𝗶 = 𝟬 → σ = 𝟬 → 𝗻𝗼 𝘀𝗲𝗽𝗮𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 The two distributions sit on top of each other. At any cutoff, AR_good = AR_bad, so the approved population is a random sample of applicants and its bad rate equals 𝗉. A zero-Gini model gives you no selection. 𝗚𝗶𝗻𝗶 = 𝟬.𝟯𝟬 → σ ≈ 𝟬.𝟱𝟰 → 𝗺𝗶𝗹𝗱 𝘀𝗲𝗽𝗮𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 The goods shift right by about half a standard deviation. Substantial overlap remains, but the right tail is richer in goods. You start catching some bads; many still slip through. 𝗚𝗶𝗻𝗶 = 𝟬.𝟱𝟬 → σ ≈ 𝟬.𝟵𝟱 → 𝗺𝗼𝗱𝗲𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗲 𝘀𝗲𝗽𝗮𝗿𝗮𝘁𝗶𝗼𝗻 Nearly one standard deviation apart. Overlap visibly reduced. At a typical cutoff, you approve a much larger share of goods than bads. Roughly where many production scorecards sit. The plot in the second image shows the loss reduction per unit of Gini improvement across acceptance rates and bad rates. Tighter acceptance gives more leverage: at 10%, all curves converge near 140% regardless of bad rate. As acceptance loosens, the curves fall and spread apart, with cleaner portfolios (lower bad rate) consistently benefiting more per Gini point than riskier ones. ▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬ The appeal of this kind of work is that it sits at the right level of abstraction.
