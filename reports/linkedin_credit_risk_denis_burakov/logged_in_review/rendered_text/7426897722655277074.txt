It is central to regulatory capital, loan pricing, and portfolio risk management. However, there is no single "right" way to model LGD, so exploring alternatives is always worth the effort. In a short paper I explore 𝘱𝘳𝘰𝘣𝘢𝘣𝘪𝘭𝘪𝘴𝘵𝘪𝘤 𝘳𝘦𝘨𝘳𝘦𝘴𝘴𝘪𝘰𝘯 𝘷𝘪𝘢 𝘲𝘶𝘢𝘯𝘵𝘪𝘭𝘦-𝘣𝘢𝘴𝘦𝘥 𝘤𝘭𝘢𝘴𝘴𝘪𝘧𝘪𝘤𝘢𝘵𝘪𝘰𝘯: instead of predicting 𝘌[𝘓𝘎𝘋|𝘟] directly, we bin LGD into quantiles, fit a multiclass classifier for 𝘗(𝘣𝘪𝘯 𝘬 | 𝘟), and take the point estimate as the probability-weighted sum of bin midpoints. This gives a full conditional distribution in one model. This approach captures LGD without normality assumptions, produces calibrated predictions, and facilitates stress testing, regulatory capital, and IFRS 9 applications.
