This can make the predictive model uneconomical 🚰 and harder to interpret. One solution is pruning entire trees. By identifying and removing trees that deviate significantly from the model’s global mean—those in the "tails" of the prediction distribution—we can shrink Random Forests while maintaining generalization and accuracy. In the slides, I illustrate how this approach works and the benefits it brings. 🌟 I also share some details about an ongoing 🐍 Python project dedicated to parsing scikit-learn Random Forest classifiers. This extends my earlier work on gradient-boosted scorecards (https://lnkd.in/dWX_RXzs) and allows you to look into the "black-box" 🔍.
