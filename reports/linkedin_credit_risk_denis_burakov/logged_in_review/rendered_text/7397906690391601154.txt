Chapter 1 explored Alan Turing and the Enigma, and Chapter 2 introduced Ronald Fisher and the birth of modern data science. The hero of Chapter 3 is HAL-9000, Stanley Kubrick's fictional AI. The foundations of machine minds like HAL were laid decades earlier by Alan Turing, whose 1948 concept of "unorganised machines" anticipated the earliest neural networks. By the mid-1950s, while AI researchers debated the nature of general intelligence at Dartmouth in 1956, the founders of FICO were already building the first scorecards, focused on interpretable, odds-based risk models. This chapter brings these two worlds together.
