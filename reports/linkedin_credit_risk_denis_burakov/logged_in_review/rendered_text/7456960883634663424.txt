The init_model trick is one of those things that looks simple enough, but solves a genuinely painful problem, nobody wants to retrain from scratch every time a new data source lands. The frozen trees point is important: original splits stay intact, new trees can use both old and new features. So you're extending the model's vocabulary without touching what it already learned. In credit risk that matters a lot, because the base model often has months of stability tracking and PSI monitoring behind it that you'd lose with a full retrain. The SHAP score tracking across base and fine-tuned trees is a nice addition too.
