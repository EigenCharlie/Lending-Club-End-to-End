Celebrated for its interpretability and historical ties to early AI theorists, WOE has stood the test of time. Yet despite its widespread use, a clear and accepted method for quantifying its uncertainty has long been the missing puzzle piece – until now. This paper fills the gap by connecting WOE with the standard errors in logistic regression. In practical terms, this allows risk practitioners to quantify the uncertainty of WOE using familiar, well-established statistical tools. 𝗧𝗟;𝗗𝗥: FastWoe is a blazing-fast Python package for WOE encoding and inference, and uncertainty quantification 🚀.
