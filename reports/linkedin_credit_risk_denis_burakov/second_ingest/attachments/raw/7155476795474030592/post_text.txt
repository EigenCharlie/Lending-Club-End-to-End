Weight-of-Evidence (WOE) = Naive Bayes? 🅿(🅰️|🅱️)
After reading through the comments to my previous post about WOE and follow-up thread in Andrija Djurovic's post, I came to a conclusion that it's a common belief that WOE's main utility is encoding 🧩. While it's one of the most well-known applications, WOE goes far beyond data preprocessing 🧪
In fact, WOE is the simplest form of a Naive Bayes model rooted in the independence assumption between risk drivers (hence "naive") 🎲. The WOE score, calculated as the logarithm of the good-to-bad ratio for a given risk group, aligns with the concept of a log-likelihood ratio in Naive Bayes models 🔮
This post aims to illustrate how Naive Bayes models can be utilized in the context of credit risk estimation 🪄, for example, through an application of Naive Bayes boosting and a technique I call "Pseudo-WOE" Naive Bayes, which allows us to build strong classification models using a powerful combination of binning and a WOE Naive Bayes to fit the observed data 🧫
