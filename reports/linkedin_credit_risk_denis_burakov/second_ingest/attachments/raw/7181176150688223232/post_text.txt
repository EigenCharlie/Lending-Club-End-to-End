Logistic Regression with Fisher Scoring ⤵
Fisher scoring is a well-known method for estimating logistic regression, developed by the innovator behind Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE). It's a widely utilized optimization framework in commercial software for fitting logistic regression models 👨💻. Its unique feature? Fisher scoring doesn't require a learning rate, taking care of the training steps automatically 🧪
