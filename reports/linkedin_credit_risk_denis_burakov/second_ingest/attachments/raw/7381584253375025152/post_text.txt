Are you interested in learning how the machine learning scorecard development process differs from traditional scorecards?
In 2023, I wrote a paper outlining a blueprint showing how constrained tree-based models can match and outperform traditional scorecard methods in discriminatory power and calibration accuracy, without losing interpretability.
As many leading institutions now transition toward explainable ML frameworks, this work provides a blueprint for the responsible integration of advanced methods within regulated environments.
