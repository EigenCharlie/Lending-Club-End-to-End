Credit Scoring with Trended Data ↗ ↘
Trended credit scores are gaining attention in the lending industry as a valuable addition to a scoring toolbox 🪛. The key premise of trended scoring lies in its ability to provide a more comprehensive view of a consumer's financial behavior compared to single-point-in-time snapshots 🎞️
The concept of modeling trended attributes is not new, and recent industry use cases have demonstrated that deep learning models excel in this task 🚀. By leveraging neural network representations of raw transactions, model Gini scores can improve by 5-10 percentage points due to the incorporation of historical trends into the models 📈
