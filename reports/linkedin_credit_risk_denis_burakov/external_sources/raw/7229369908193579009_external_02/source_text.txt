Gradient Boosting ✨
This post continues the series covering common tools and instruments to build risk models. Gradient boosting, and most notably gradient boosted decision tree (GBDT), is a powerful instrument in a modeller's toolkit for problems like default prediction 💳
The deck builds upon blogs by one of scikit-learn's core developers and offers a #Python 🐍 implementation of gradient boosting from scratch. Crafted for clarity, this post introduces gradient boosting from the ground up, offering a hands-on approach to this powerful modeling technique 🔮
