How to Build a Credit Scorecard Using Naïve Bayes? 🎲
This text continues the well-received series on Weight of Evidence (WOE), a beloved tool of many risk model builders around the world 🌎
Discover the history and application of WOE, tracing back to Alan Turing and his statistical assistant, I.J. Good, who conceptualized WOE as a log-likelihood ratio. This post demystifies Good's simplified WOE formula, not commonly applied in #CreditRisk, to perform inference and construct scorecards. This text offers a step-by-step journey through scorecard creation with Naïve Bayes, illustrated for clear understanding 💡
