Weighted Logistic Regression ⇅
Weighted logistic regression is a method for addressing data imbalances in scenarios such as credit scoring for new financial products 💳 and can be used for adjusting the balance between the costs of incorrect predictions 🔍. By modifying the cost weight of each class (e.g., non-default or default), it directly impacts the consequences of misclassification by the model ⚖️
This approach can be useful for extremely imbalanced datasets (< 5% positive event rate), which are common in areas like secured lending 🏦 and fraud monitoring 🕵️♂️. Through practical examples in credit approval, we demonstrate how models can be refined for improved risk assessment using logistic regression 🧬 and gradient boosted trees 🌳
