Here are 10 powerful Python tricks every beginner (and even working professional) should know 👇
✅ 𝟭. 𝗦𝘄𝗮𝗽 𝘁𝘄𝗼 𝘃𝗮𝗿𝗶𝗮𝗯𝗹𝗲𝘀 𝗶𝗻𝘀𝘁𝗮𝗻𝘁𝗹𝘆
