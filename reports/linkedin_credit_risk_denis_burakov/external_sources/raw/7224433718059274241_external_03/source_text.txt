Logistic Regression 🧬
There was a time, not too distant, when the notion of open-source software rivaling proprietary analytics vendors elicited outright laughter at risk conferences. Those days seem long gone and today, #Python 🐍 and its dynamic developer community have democratized access to modeling algorithms, once hidden behind hefty license fees 🧾
In this post, we will dive deep into Logistic Regression, the most common algorithm used for binary classification according to recent years’ Kaggle surveys 📊. In this post, we cover two major implementations of the Logistic Regression algorithm: Gradient Descent and Iterative Reweighted Least Squares (IRLS) 🚀
Whether you’re a seasoned data developer or just curious about how the most widely used algorithm underpins contemporary risk modeling practice, this exploration offers insights into the inner workings of Logistic Regression 🧩
