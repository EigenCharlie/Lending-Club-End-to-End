Log Loss 📉
Log loss, also known as logistic loss or cross-entropy loss, is a common metric used in model training and evaluation of #MachineLearning models working with binary outcomes (e.g., credit default vs. no credit default). Log Loss measures how fit a model is for a dataset and penalizes the model's confidence in incorrect predictions 🎯
This deck traces the metric's origins back to the concept of likelihood from #Statistics, shares examples of binomial and bernoulli logistic regression in #Python 🐍, and visually explains this metric based on examples with gradient-boosted decision trees 🌳
