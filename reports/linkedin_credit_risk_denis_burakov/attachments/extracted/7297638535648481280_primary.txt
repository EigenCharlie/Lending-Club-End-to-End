Application of the approach to real-world data

In this example, we use a real-world dataset
(breast cancer) to visualize the 95%
confidence intervals for four well-established
algorithms for binary classification:

•   Logistic Regression
•   Naïve Bayes
•   SVM
•   Random Forest

The plots illustrate how the intervals reflect
the actual coverage of points above or below
the predicted probabilities, allowing us to
evaluate and compare models that produce
probability-like scores

While this experimental approach is not a
classical case of conformal prediction (CP), it
shares a similar objective of quantifying
uncertainty using established methodologies
from both machine learning (CP) and
traditional statistics (discrepancy measures)
