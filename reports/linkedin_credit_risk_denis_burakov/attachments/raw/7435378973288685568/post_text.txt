A few things that stuck with me:
Marie from Piano gave an interesting number that $12.9B is lost annually to bad data quality, and 60% of dashboards nobody uses.
Visible wins in analytics accelerate adoption faster than training in data literacy.
Cloud data sovereignty is heating up in the EU. With hyperscalers holding sensitive data, the regulatory and trust questions around EU cloud are becoming impossible to ignore.
And some really clever methodology I noted down about churn analytics: combining quantitative data with open qualitative exit responses of users gives LLMs some decent ground to come up with experiment hypotheses.
