New chapter on batch scoring and credit limit management, a core workflow for anyone managing revolving credit products.
Banks and FinTechs need to periodically re-score existing customers and adjust credit limits based on behavioral patterns. This normally happens at scale (thousands of customers), on schedule (daily/monthly), and typically requires separating data science work from credit policy decisions.
The chapter covers AWS services that help orchestrate this process: EventBridge, SageMaker, Lambda, and Redshift.
As always, everything runs locally with Docker (now using 𝘥𝘰𝘤𝘬𝘦𝘳-𝘱𝘺 so it's all in Python) and LocalStack for hands-on learning.
More AWS chapters coming before the credit scoring book release 📕. Next up: SageMaker MLflow experiments.
