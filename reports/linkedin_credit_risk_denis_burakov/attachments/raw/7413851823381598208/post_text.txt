We're back, this time with updates to the practitioner's guide to Credit Risk Modeling on AWS. The first guide of its kind is shaping up to help more practitioners get hands-on with cloud workflows without the hasty bills 💸
What is this about, and who is it for?
The project uses Docker, Inc, LocalStack, and Amazon SageMaker to deliver real-world examples of modern credit risk work. Everything runs locally and for free and yet allows you to implement common use cases without worrying about billing statement emails ✉️
If you're curious about credit risk data science on AWS in a practical and cost-conscious way, this guide is being built for you!
