Linear Minds in a Nonlinear World 🧠🌍
The key challenge of every predictive modeling project lies in finding a model that accurately describes the underlying data patterns 📉
On one end of the spectrum, flexible models (think ensemble methods 🌳) can easily memorize noise in the training data — a common issue known as overfitting. On the other, simpler models (like linear or logistic regression 📏) may overlook more complex relationships, leading to underfitting
How do we find a balance between the two extremes ❓
The answer lies in the bias-variance tradeoff — a "sweet spot" where a model captures the right amount of complexity without overfitting or underfitting. Explore how techniques like error curve analysis and bias-variance decomposition can be used to find the right model ⤵
