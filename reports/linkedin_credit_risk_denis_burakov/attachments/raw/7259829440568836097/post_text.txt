Adversarial Learning with Iris🪻
How can neural networks create images that look almost real❓
Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) make this possible by pitting two models against each other: a generator that creates images from scratch and a discriminator that tries to tell them apart from real ones 🖼️
Through adversarial learning, the generator's images become nearly indistinguishable from reality ✨
Check out this fun experiment generating Iris flower images, along with a visual breakdown of how GAN training works ⤵
