🧩 Multiclass Weight of Evidence (WOE) In many risk modeling problems, we deal with two outcomes (Default vs No Default, Fraud vs No Fraud), which makes log-odds modeling and  and the use of Weight of Evidence (WOE) straightforward. But what happens when there are more than two outcomes? I’ve been exploring how to extend the WOE framework to multiclass settings, where "yes vs no" comparisons no longer apply cleanly. In this new deck, I look at two complementary approaches for multiclass inference using likelihood ratios and WOE — one based on simple hypotheses and another on simple vs composite ones (after I.J. Good). This will appear in my upcoming chapter: 📘𝘓𝘰𝘨𝘪𝘴𝘵𝘪𝘤 𝘙𝘦𝘨𝘳𝘦𝘴𝘴𝘪𝘰𝘯: 𝘍𝘳𝘰𝘮 𝘔𝘢𝘹𝘪𝘮𝘶𝘮 𝘓𝘪𝘬𝘦𝘭𝘪𝘩𝘰𝘰𝘥 𝘵𝘰 𝘔𝘢𝘤𝘩𝘪𝘯𝘦 𝘓𝘦𝘢𝘳𝘯𝘪𝘯𝘨 Update: Thanks to Rahul Shaw for spotting an error in Table on page 7.
