🧩 Multiclass Weight of Evidence (WOE)
In many risk modeling problems, we deal with two outcomes (Default vs No Default, Fraud vs No Fraud), which makes log-odds modeling and and the use of Weight of Evidence (WOE) straightforward.
But what happens when there are more than two outcomes?
I’ve been exploring how to extend the WOE framework to multiclass settings, where "yes vs no" comparisons no longer apply cleanly.
In this new deck, I look at two complementary approaches for multiclass inference using likelihood ratios and WOE — one based on simple hypotheses and another on simple vs composite ones (after I.J. Good).
