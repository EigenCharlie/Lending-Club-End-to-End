Had an amazing Sunday attending Cafe Cursor in Berlin ☕
What keeps striking me these days: while LinkedIn is full of "AI can't do X / AI isn't good enough for Y" posts, this room was packed with builders quietly pushing boundaries and exploring what's actually possible with these tools. Thanks to the organizers and presenters, especially for the Q&A covering context management, Cursor rules, subagents and other exciting topics.
It's really about mindset. You can spend time learning how to use these tools to create value, or you can spend time listing what they can't do. The same tool that seems limited to one person can become incredibly powerful in the hands of someone who's invested the time to learn it.
