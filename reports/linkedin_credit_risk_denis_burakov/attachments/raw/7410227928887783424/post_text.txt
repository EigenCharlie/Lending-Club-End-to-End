In earlier chapters, we explored foundational figures like Alan Turing, Jack Good, and Ronald Fisher, whose work shaped modern data science.
This chapter focuses on a less celebrated but equally transformative figure: Leo Breiman, whose "statistics in the style of punk" revolutionized machine learning for tabular data.
We trace the evolution from decision trees to ensemble methods (Random Forests and Gradient Boosting), moving to SHAP values and their application to credit risk through TreeSHAP, SHAP scorecards, and distillation.
This is the most technical chapter, but conceptual explanations take priority over code. I hope you enjoy this final chapter's pre-print during the break. The full book arrives in 2026!
