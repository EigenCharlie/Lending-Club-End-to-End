# Paper 4 Deep Cleanup Memo - 2026-05-18

## Decision

Claude's minimum cleanup recommendation was directionally right but too
conservative for the actual project state. It identified orphan CSVs and the
one-swap loop, but left most generated v39-v466 and v479-v489 artifacts in place
only because some guardrail tests still mentioned them.

Those tests were preserving the loop rather than protecting the paper. The
cleanup now keeps the evidence that contributes to the Quarto book, Paper
Estrella, or provenance, and removes generated artifacts that no longer carry a
claim.

## Keep

- Base/unversioned Paper 4 governance tables.
- v1-v38 Paper 4 evidence read by the Quarto book and official synthesis.
- v467-v478 curated export/provenance outputs for F03/F04/F05/SDAM and final
  section-stub planning.
- 2026-05-17 loop-closure artifacts that document the accepted four-chapter
  patch and the v490-v526 deletion.
- 2026-05-18 deep-cleanup manifest as the auditable deletion record.

## Delete

- v39-v466 Paper 4 tables, status JSONs and notes.
- v479-v489 Paper 4 tables, status JSONs and notes.
- v39-v489 one-shot builders under `scripts/papers/`.
- Stale `__pycache__` files generated by those deleted builders.
- The old 56k-line guardrail test body that encoded the loop as if every wave
  had equal long-term value.

official publication/champion surface. No Paper 4 artifact in this cleanup
promotes a new champion, reopens the final promotion protocol, or creates
`paper4_final_promotion.json`.
